READING THE NUMBERS

A clear rule. A fair comparison.

Keep sales of $10,000 or more

Sales below $10,000 are excluded from both the counts and medians because the dataset contains implausibly low recorded prices. Sales exactly at $10,000 are included. No upper price cutoff is applied.

The middle sale, not the average

For each city and state, prices are sorted and the middle value is the median. For an even count, it is the average of the two middle prices. Prices are displayed rounded to the nearest whole US dollar; ranking uses the unrounded median.

Sales, not unique addresses

Each qualifying row counts as a sale. Repeated street addresses are kept: they may represent separate units or sales. No filters are applied to dates, home type, size, or other property fields.

A snapshot of the source

These are completed sales in the collection, not all sales in each market or current asking prices. Values are as reported by the collection source and have not been verified against county records.
